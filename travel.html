Travel Guide

Getting here, getting around, and when to go.

How to Reach Karnataka

Major airports: Bengaluru (BLR), Mangaluru, Hubballi, Mysuru and Belagavi.
Bengaluru, Mysuru, Hubballi and Mangaluru are the main railway hubs with links across India.
KSRTC and private operators connect Bengaluru to nearly every town in the state.
National highways link Karnataka to Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Goa, Maharashtra and Andhra Pradesh.

Getting Around

Namma Metro covers Bengaluru's main corridors. Best for city travel.

BMTC in Bengaluru and KSRTC between towns. Cheap and frequent.

App cabs work in cities. For day trips, book a driver by the day.

Self-drive cars and bikes are popular in Coorg, Gokarna and Hampi. Carry your licence.

Best Time to Visit

Winter

Oct – Feb

Best overall: cool, clear, ideal for heritage and beaches.

Summer

Mar – May

Hot inland. Head to hill stations and coffee country.

Monsoon

Jun – Sep

Waterfalls at full flow and lush Ghats. Roads can be slippery.
